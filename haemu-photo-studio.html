Summary

  • The App is a fully offline story game. It does not collect, store on a server, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device data.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud save, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, files, location, installed apps, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, analytics, telemetry, usage statistics, or crash reports. The App makes no network requests to us or to any third party.

2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue your game, the App saves the following locally in the App’s private storage on your device: game progress (save slots, unlocked endings and album images, read-text history), the character name you type in, and your settings (volume, text speed, language). This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

3. Deleting your data

Because everything is stored only on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the App, or by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Haemu Photo Studio → Storage → Clear data). We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

4. Permissions

The App does not request any runtime permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage access).

5. Advertising, analytics, and crash reporting

The App contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no crash-reporting services.

6. Purchases

The App is sold as a one-time paid download through Google Play. Payment is processed entirely by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy; we never receive your payment details. The App has no in-app purchases.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a romance story intended for teens and adults and is not directed to children under 13. In any case, the App collects no personal information from anyone.

8. Security

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no data in transit or on our servers to protect. Data saved on your device is protected by your device’s own app sandboxing.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before that version is released.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: purplusnow@gmail.com

Summary

  • The game itself plays fully offline. Your progress, saves, and the character name you type are kept only on your own device.
  • From version 1.1.0, the App sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase so that we can see how far players get in the story and fix problems. These do not include your name, email, the character name you type, or your advertising ID.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud save, and no advertising. We never sell or share data for advertising.

1. What we collect

The App uses two Google services. Both are tied only to a random identifier generated for this installation of the App (a Firebase installation / app instance ID), not to your name or Google account.

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Google Analytics for Firebase In-game actions and progress (for example: which day of the story you reached, which places you visited, which choices you made, photos taken, endings reached, play time, settings changed), app version, device model, OS version, language, approximate country (derived from the IP address, which is not stored), and session timing. To understand how players enjoy the story (where they stop, which routes are popular, which endings are reached) and to improve the game.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash and error reports: stack traces, app version, device model, OS version, and the time of the crash. To find and fix crashes.

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, files, precise location, installed apps, or the advertising ID (the App removes the advertising ID permission and turns off ad personalization signals). The character name you type is never sent.

Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase Privacy and Security terms. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising.

2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue your game, the App saves the following locally in the App’s private storage on your device: game progress (save slots, unlocked endings and album images, read-text history), the character name you type in, and your settings (volume, text speed, language). This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

2a. How long we keep collected data

Usage statistics are kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

3. Deleting your data

Your game data is stored only on your device: delete it at any time by uninstalling the App, or by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Haemu Photo Studio → Storage → Clear data). Clearing data or reinstalling also gives the App a new random identifier, so new statistics are no longer linked to the old ones.

Because the statistics and crash reports are linked only to that random identifier and not to your identity, we cannot pick out one person’s records on request; they are deleted automatically at the end of the retention period above. For any question about your data, email us at purplusnow@gmail.com.

4. Permissions

The App does not request any runtime permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage access). It uses the standard Internet and network-state permissions only to send the usage statistics and crash reports described above; the game works without a connection, and statistics are sent later when one is available.

5. Advertising

The App contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs, and it does not use the advertising ID.

6. Purchases

The App is sold as a one-time paid download through Google Play. Payment is processed entirely by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy; we never receive your payment details. The App has no in-app purchases.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a romance story intended for teens and adults and is not directed to children under 13. In any case, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the statistics described above contain no name or contact details.

8. Security

Usage statistics and crash reports are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to Google Firebase, and access to them is limited to our team. Data saved on your device is protected by your device’s own app sandboxing.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before that version is released.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: purplusnow@gmail.com

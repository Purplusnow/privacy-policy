If you choose to link a Google account for cloud backup, we also process the identifiers described in section 4. Linking is optional and the App is fully playable without it.

3. How Information May Be Used

  • to measure app usage, story progression, game balance, and general engagement so we can improve the game
  • to diagnose and fix crashes and performance problems
  • to verify and restore the full-version purchase
  • to back up and restore your game saves
  • to read feedback you choose to send from the App’s settings and improve the game

4. Cloud Backup and Google Sign-In

Your save files are stored on your device. The App also uploads a backup copy to Cloud Firestore (hosted in Seoul, South Korea) so that your progress can be restored after a reinstall or on a new device. Access is restricted so that an account can read and write only its own save.

Cloud backup first uses an anonymous Firebase account, which is not tied to your identity. If you sign in with Google (Settings → Account & backup → Link Google account), the save is linked to your Google account identifier so it can be restored on another device. We receive an account identifier, your email address (shown in the App so you can see which account is linked) and a sign-in token from Google. We do not receive your Google password. You can sign out at any time in Settings → Account & backup → Sign out; your progress stays on the device.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings → Send feedback, the text you write is stored in Cloud Firestore together with the app version, your language, your device model and OS version, your current week in the game, whether the full version is unlocked, the time it was sent, and your account identifier, so we can read it and fix problems in later updates. Do not include personal information you do not want us to have. Feedback can only be written by the App and is not readable by other players.

5. Advertising

The App contains no advertising and no advertising SDK, and it does not use your advertising identifier.

6. In-App Purchase

The App can be played for free up to a point in the story. A single one-time purchase unlocks the full version; it has fixed, listed contents and there are no randomized paid items or other purchases. Payment is processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details. The unlock is stored on your device, and the App checks your purchases with Google Play when it starts, so the full version is restored after a reinstall. You can also use Settings → Restore Purchase.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by the third-party services listed in section 2 only as necessary to provide analytics, crash reporting, billing, and cloud backup.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account and its cloud backup at any time in the App: Settings → Account & backup → Delete account. It removes the cloud save from Cloud Firestore and deletes the Firebase account. Progress stored on your device stays until you delete the App.

Local settings and your save files remain on your device until you delete the App. A cloud backup is kept until you delete it in the App or ask us to delete it. To request deletion of your cloud backup or feedback (including for an anonymous account), email purplusnow@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, or from any address together with enough detail to identify the save. Data held by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will review and delete it.

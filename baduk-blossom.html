Summary

  • Games are played offline. The Go engine runs on your device; no board position, move, or game is ever sent anywhere for analysis.
  • The App backs up your save data to our cloud so you can keep your record if you change devices. That backup is tied to an account identifier created automatically on first launch.
  • Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, we store your email address so the account can be shown and recovered.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising or device identifier of any kind.
  • You can delete your account and its cloud copy from inside the App, or by email — see Deleting your account and data.

2. Who processes it

The backup is stored in our own project on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), acting as our data processor. Data is transmitted over HTTPS and is encrypted in transit. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data, and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. Google’s own handling of the infrastructure is governed by the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation.

3. Playing offline

The App ships with its Go engine and neural network inside the application package, and all move analysis, scoring, and rank estimation happen on your device. You can play complete games with no network connection. Nothing about your games is sent anywhere for analysis — the only thing that leaves your device is the save backup described above.

4. Deleting your account and data

You can do this yourself, at any time, in two ways:

  • In the App — open Settings → Account → Delete account and cloud data. After you confirm, the cloud copy and the account are deleted, and the App stops creating a new one. The games stored on your own device are kept, and you can keep playing.
  • By email — if you have already uninstalled the App, write to purplusnow@gmail.com with the subject “Go Blossom account deletion”. Tell us the email address you signed in with, or, if you never signed in, the approximate date you installed the App and your in-game rank, so we can locate the record. We reply within 30 days and confirm once the data is removed.

Deletion removes the account identifier, the email address if one was stored, and the save backup. Nothing is retained afterwards. We keep the backup only while the account exists; there is no separate archive or backup retention period beyond the normal operation of the service.

6. Advertising, analytics, and crash reporting

The App contains no advertising and no advertising or attribution SDK, no analytics, and no crash reporting. We receive no usage data from the App.

7. Purchases

If the App offers an optional subscription, that purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment details — such as card numbers and billing addresses — are handled by Google and are never seen or stored by the App or by us. The App only learns whether an active entitlement exists so it can unlock the corresponding features. Google’s handling of that transaction is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Signing in with Google is optional and is the only way an email address is ever stored. If you believe a child has signed in and you would like the account and its data removed, use either route in Deleting your account and data and we will remove it.

9. Security

Save data on your device lives in the App’s private storage area, protected by the Android operating system’s app sandbox. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. The cloud copy is readable only by the account it belongs to, enforced by Firebase Authentication and security rules.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its processing. The App itself shows you everything we store about you — it is your own game record — and the deletion routes above cover erasure. For anything else, email purplusnow@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes what is collected, we will update this page before that version is published and change the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be noted in the App’s store listing release notes.

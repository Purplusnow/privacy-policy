Summary

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It runs entirely on your device. The App does not contain advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or any account system, and it does not request the Android INTERNET permission — so it is technically unable to send information anywhere.

1. Information We Collect

We collect none. Specifically, the App does not collect or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other contact information
  • Account identifiers — the App has no sign-in, registration, or user profile
  • Advertising identifiers — the App contains no advertising SDK
  • Analytics, telemetry, usage statistics, or crash reports
  • Location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, files, calendar, or call/SMS data
  • Device identifiers of any kind

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves your game progress locally so you can continue where you left off. This save file stays on your device and is never uploaded. It contains only game data:

  • Your in-game rank estimate, game results, and per-move scores
  • An unfinished game in progress, so it can be resumed
  • App settings such as sound, coordinate display, and language

You can erase all of this at any time by uninstalling the App, or through your device’s Settings → Apps → Baduk Blossom → Storage → Clear data. Because nothing is uploaded, there is no server-side copy for us to delete and no deletion request to make.

4. Advertising

The App contains no advertising, and no advertising or attribution SDK of any kind.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The App contains no analytics and no crash reporting. We receive no usage data from the App.

6. Purchases

If the App offers an optional subscription, that purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment details — such as card numbers and billing addresses — are handled by Google and are never seen or stored by the App or by us. The App only learns whether an active entitlement exists so it can unlock the corresponding features. Google’s handling of that transaction is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect any. No data leaves your device.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected, no parental consent mechanism or data-deletion request is required.

9. Security

Your game data stays in the App’s private storage area on your device, protected by the Android operating system’s app sandbox. Since no data is transmitted, there is no transmission to secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the App introduces a feature that collects or transmits data, we will update this page before that version is published and change the “Last updated” date above. Material changes will also be noted in the App’s store listing release notes.

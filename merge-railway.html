4. Cloud Backup and Google Sign-In

Your save file is stored on your device. The App also uploads a backup copy to Cloud Firestore so that your progress can be restored after a reinstall or on a new device. Access is restricted so that an account can read and write only its own save.

Cloud backup first uses an anonymous Firebase account, which is not tied to your identity. If you sign in with Google (Settings → Cloud Backup → Back up with Google), the save is linked to your Google account identifier so it can be restored on another device. We receive an account identifier, your email address (shown in the App so you can see which account is linked) and a sign-in token from Google. We do not receive your Google password. You can log out at any time in Settings → Cloud Backup → Log out; your progress stays on the device.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings → Feedback, the text you write is stored in Cloud Firestore together with the app version, your language, your current level and train number, the time it was sent, and your account identifier, so we can read it and fix problems in later updates. Do not include personal information you do not want us to have. Feedback can only be written by the App and is not readable by other players.

5. Notifications

The App can show local reminders (for example when your energy is full, when the daily reward is ready, or a reminder about the train you are restoring). They are scheduled on your device; no notification data is sent to us or to a push server. You can turn them off in Settings → Notifications or in your device settings.

6. Advertising

The App shows only rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, and only when you tap a button to watch one. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and app interaction data in accordance with Google’s policies. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the App asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

7. In-App Purchases

The App offers in-app purchases of gem packs, an energy pack, a one-time starter pack, and the one-time Engineer Pass. Payment is processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details. We store the resulting entitlement on your device (and in your cloud backup) and verify the purchase receipt to prevent fraudulent purchases. The Engineer Pass can be restored with Settings → Restore purchases.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by the third-party services listed in section 2 only as necessary to provide advertising, analytics, crash reporting, billing, and cloud backup.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your account and its cloud backup at any time in the App: Settings → Cloud Backup → Delete account (shown while a Google account is linked). It removes the cloud save from Cloud Firestore and deletes the Firebase account. Progress stored on your device stays until you delete the App.

Local settings and your save file remain on your device until you delete the App. A cloud backup is kept until you delete it in the App or ask us to delete it. To request deletion of your cloud backup or feedback (including for an anonymous account), email purplusnow@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, or from any address together with enough detail to identify the save. Data held by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will review and delete it.

4. Cloud Save and Google Sign-In

Your save file is stored on your device. The App can additionally upload a backup copy to Cloud Firestore so that your progress can be restored after a reinstall or on a new device. Access is restricted so that an account can read and write only its own save.

Cloud save first uses an anonymous Firebase account, which is not tied to your identity. If you sign in with Google, the save is linked to your Google account identifier so it can be restored on another device. We receive an account identifier and sign-in token from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

5. Advertising

The App may show rewarded video ads through Google AdMob when you choose to watch one. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and app interaction data in accordance with Google’s policies. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

6. In-App Purchases

The App offers in-app purchases of in-game gems. Payment is processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details. We store the resulting entitlement on your device and verify the purchase receipt to prevent fraudulent purchases.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by the third-party services listed in section 2 only as necessary to provide advertising, analytics, billing, and cloud save.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local settings and your save file remain on your device until you delete the App. A cloud save backup is kept until you ask us to delete it. To request deletion of your cloud save, email purplusnow@gmail.com from the address linked to your account, or from any address together with enough detail to identify the save. Data held by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will review and delete it.

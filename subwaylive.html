1. Information We Collect

The App works without an account or sign-in. We do not collect your name, address, phone number, contacts or payment card details, and the App does not request your location. Your favorite and recent stations and your settings are stored only on your device. The App uses the services listed below, which may collect device, usage, advertising and purchase-related information.

3. How Information May Be Used

  • to show live arrival times for the stations you open
  • to show the banner ad and to respect your ad-removal purchase
  • to measure general app usage so we can improve the App
  • to diagnose and fix crashes
  • to verify and restore the ad-removal purchase, and to detect tampered or forged receipts

4. Feedback

If you send feedback from Settings → Send feedback, we receive the text you write together with the app version, language, theme, device model and operating system version, so we can understand the report. It is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Seoul region) under an anonymous identifier that is created only when you first send feedback. Please do not include personal information in feedback.

5. Advertising

The App shows one banner ad through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location and app interaction data in accordance with Google’s policies. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the App asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the App’s settings. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

6. In-App Purchase

The App offers a single one-time purchase that removes ads permanently. Payment is processed by Google Play Billing; we never receive or store your payment card details. The entitlement is stored on your device and can be restored from Settings.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by the services listed in section 2 only as necessary to provide live transit data, advertising, analytics, crash reporting and billing.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not create an account, so there is no account to delete. Favorites, recent stations and settings stay on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. To ask us to delete feedback you sent, email purplusnow@gmail.com with enough detail to identify it. Data held by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies.

9. Public Data Sources

Live arrival data: Seoul Open Data Plaza (Korea Open Government License Type 1). Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Arrival times are estimates and may differ from actual service.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will review and delete it.

11. Your Choices

  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • You can remove the App from your device at any time.

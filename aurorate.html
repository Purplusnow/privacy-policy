1. Information We Collect

Aurorate is a currency converter and calculator. It has no user accounts and no sign-in, and we do not collect your name, address, phone number, email address or payment card details. The amounts you type, your list of currencies and your settings are stored only on your device; they are never uploaded to us.

The App uses the third-party services listed below, which may collect device, usage, advertising and purchase-related information.

If you choose to send feedback from Settings → Send feedback, we store the text you write together with the App version, the App language and device language, your device model and operating system version, the list of currency codes you follow, and an anonymous Firebase account identifier, so that we can understand and fix the issue. Please do not include personal information in your message. Sending feedback is optional and we do not reply to it.

3. How Information May Be Used

  • to show the banner ad and measure its delivery
  • to measure app usage and general engagement so we can improve the App
  • to diagnose and fix crashes
  • to verify and restore the “Remove ads” purchase, and to detect forged purchase receipts
  • to read and act on feedback you choose to send

4. Exchange Rates

Rates are mid-market reference rates from public sources and are provided for information only. They are not an offer to exchange money and may differ from the rate your bank or card provider applies. Nothing in the App is financial advice.

5. Advertising

The App shows one banner ad through Google AdMob at the bottom of the screen. It does not show full-screen or video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location and app interaction data in accordance with Google’s policies. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the App asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings. Buying “Remove ads” removes the banner.

6. In-App Purchases

The App offers one optional in-app purchase, “Remove ads”, a one-time purchase that removes the banner ad permanently and is restored on reinstall. Payment is processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by the third-party services listed in section 2 only as necessary to provide exchange rates, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, billing and feedback.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your currencies, amounts and settings stay on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. We hold no account or cloud copy of them.

Feedback messages are kept for up to two years to help us improve the App and are then deleted. To have your feedback deleted earlier, email purplusnow@gmail.com with the approximate date and content of your message. Data held by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will review and delete it.

10. Your Choices

  • You can use every feature without signing in, and without sending feedback.
  • You can remove the banner ad with the “Remove ads” purchase.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • You can remove the App from your device at any time.

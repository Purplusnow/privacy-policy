1. Your Photos and Videos Stay on Your Phone

FeelCam is a film-look camera. Photos and videos you take are processed entirely on your device and saved to your phone’s gallery (the Pictures/FeelCam and Movies/FeelCam folders). We never upload, receive or see your photos, videos or microphone recordings. There is no account to create.

When a free user takes a photo with a Pro feature, the App also keeps the original capture in its private storage on your device (at most the 100 most recent) so the watermark can be removed if you buy FeelCam Pro. These files never leave your phone and are deleted when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.

If you choose Edit a photo, Android’s photo picker lets you select one photo; the App can read only the photo you select and does not request access to your whole photo library.

4. Feedback

If you choose to send feedback from Settings → Send feedback, we store the text you write together with the App version, the App language, your device model and operating system version, whether you have FeelCam Pro, and an anonymous account identifier, so that we can understand and fix the issue. Please do not include personal information in your message. Sending feedback is optional and we do not reply to it.

5. How Information May Be Used

  • to show the banner ad and measure its delivery
  • to measure app usage and general engagement (for example which filters are popular) so we can improve the App
  • to diagnose and fix crashes and performance problems
  • to verify and restore the FeelCam Pro purchase, and to detect forged purchase receipts
  • to read and act on feedback you choose to send

6. Advertising

The App shows one banner ad through Google AdMob at the bottom of the screen. It does not show full-screen or video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location and app interaction data in accordance with Google’s policies. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the App asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings. FeelCam Pro removes the banner.

7. In-App Purchase

The App is free with a set of free filters. It offers one optional in-app purchase, FeelCam Pro, a one-time purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks all premium filters and effects, removes the watermark and the banner ad, and is restored on reinstall. Payment is processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by the third-party services listed in section 3 only as necessary to provide advertising, analytics, crash reporting, billing and feedback.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Photos and videos are stored in your phone’s gallery and stay until you delete them; deleting the App does not delete them. The App’s settings and kept originals stay until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Feedback messages are kept for up to two years and are then deleted; to have your feedback deleted earlier, email purplusnow@gmail.com with the approximate date and content of your message. Data held by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will review and delete it.

11. Your Choices

  • You can use every feature without an account, and without sending feedback.
  • You can decline microphone access; videos are then recorded without sound.
  • You can remove the banner ad and watermark with FeelCam Pro.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • You can remove the App from your device at any time; your photos stay in your gallery.

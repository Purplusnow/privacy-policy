1. Information We Collect

Pillbloom is a medication reminder and health record app. You can use every feature without creating an account. The medicines you add (names, strengths, appearance, schedules and notes), your dose records, supply counts, the health measurements you enter (such as blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, temperature, heart rate and oxygen saturation), day notes and mood, family profile names, and your settings are stored on your device. This is health information: we treat it as sensitive, never sell it, never use it for advertising and never send it to analytics. We do not collect your name, address, phone number or payment card details.

To protect your records if your phone is lost or reset, the App keeps a backup copy in Cloud Firestore under an anonymous account (see section 4). The App also uses the third-party services listed below, which may collect device, usage, advertising and purchase-related information.

If you choose to send feedback from Settings → Send feedback, we store the text you write together with the App version, the App language, your device model and operating system version, the number of medicines and profiles you have, and your anonymous account identifier, so that we can understand and fix the issue. Please do not include personal information in your message. Sending feedback is optional and we do not reply to it.

4. Backup and Google Sign-In

Your records are stored on your device. The App also uploads a backup copy to Cloud Firestore so they can be restored after a reinstall or on a new device. The backup contains health information and is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google Cloud. Access is restricted so that an account can read and write only its own backup; nobody else, including other users, can read it.

The backup first uses an anonymous Firebase account, which is not tied to your identity. If you connect a Google account in Settings → Backup, the backup is linked to your Google account identifier so it can be restored on another phone. We receive an account identifier, your email address (shown in the App so you can see which account is connected) and a sign-in token from Google. We do not receive your Google password. You can sign out at any time; your records stay on the device.

5. Reminders

Medicine and refill reminders are scheduled on your device by Android. No reminder data is sent to us or to a push server. You can turn reminders off in your device’s notification settings.

Not a medical device

Pillbloom helps you remember and record your medicines. It is not a medical device, does not give medical advice and does not check doses or interactions. Always follow your doctor or pharmacist.

6. Advertising

The App shows one banner ad through Google AdMob at the bottom of the screen. It does not show full-screen or video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location and app interaction data in accordance with Google’s policies. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the App asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings. Buying “Remove ads” removes the banner.

7. In-App Purchases

Every feature of the App is free. The App offers one optional in-app purchase, “Remove ads”, a one-time purchase that removes the banner ad permanently and is restored on reinstall. Payment is processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by the third-party services listed in section 2 only as necessary to provide backup, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, billing and feedback.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Records on your device stay until you delete them, clear the App’s data or uninstall it. The cloud backup is kept while your account exists.

  • Connected Google account: delete your account and backup in the App from Settings → Backup → Delete account. This removes the cloud backup and the account; records on your phone stay.
  • Without a connected account: email purplusnow@gmail.com and we will delete the anonymous backup. Tell us the approximate date you installed the App and, if you can, the number of medicines you had, so we can find it.

Feedback messages are kept for up to two years to help us improve the App and are then deleted. To have your feedback deleted earlier, email us with the approximate date and content of your message. Data held by third-party providers is retained according to their own policies.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will review and delete it.
